# Roadmap

## North star
Become an Enterprise Automation & AI Transformation Architect who connects business outcomes, integration architecture, cloud platforms, security, operations, and AI-enabled delivery.

## Phase 1 — Automation Architect Foundation
**Target: 31 December 2026**

Master REST APIs, authentication, integration patterns, Python automation, Azure and ServiceNow fundamentals, observability, ITIL, and secure delivery. Publish notes, diagrams, labs, and interview explanations.

## Phase 2 — AI Automation Architect
**Target: 31 December 2027**

Design AI-assisted automation with human controls and measurable outcomes; build resilient event-driven systems; lead technical design discussions.

## Phase 3 — Transformation Leader
**Target: 31 December 2028**

Lead enterprise transformation roadmaps, technical governance, and business-case conversations.

## 2029–2030 — Architect at scale
Deepen executive communication, platform strategy, and cross-functional transformation leadership while maintaining a public body of architecture evidence.
